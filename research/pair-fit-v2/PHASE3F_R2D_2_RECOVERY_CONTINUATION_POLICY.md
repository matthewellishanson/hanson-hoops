# Phase 3F-R2D.2 recovery continuation policy

## Authority and stop boundary

This checkpoint may revalidate the original quarantined Indiana Early Base body offline, make exactly one sequential attempt for each audited R2D.1 continuation identity at original ordinals 2–8, reconcile Indiana and Memphis pair populations, and assign the frozen R2C.1 team dispositions. Indiana Early Base is network-prohibited. No other request is authorized.

The transport contract is the established research headers, `trust_env=False`, redirects disabled, a 30-second timeout, zero retries, one attempt per identity, original ordinal order, and at least one full monotonic second from an attempt’s completion to the next attempt’s start. Each start, response, verification, and outcome is immutable. Any ambiguous prior state, transport failure, redirect, non-200 response, echo mismatch, structural/schema/pair/value failure, hash failure, or persistence failure stops the sequence; a failed identity is quarantined and is not retried.

The sole response-echo comparator is R2D.1 `compare_response_echo`: strict calendar equivalence for dates, only the frozen `PORound=""` to JSON integer `0` exception, only its named numeric and empty/null equivalences, and only `ISTRound=null` as an extra field. The response envelope must be ordered `Overall`, then `Lineups`; Base and Advanced populations must contain unique valid canonical two-player keys. Zero-possession rows remain evidence. Advanced possession and rating fields must be finite numeric values.

## Evidence and dispositions

The original R2D failure, quarantine, and Indiana body remain unchanged. The R2D.2 offline verification record references the original body and hashes; it does not copy, move, rewrite, delete, or promote it. Both R2D.1 namespace lineage fields remain intact: `future_output_namespace` is historical R2C.1 lineage and `continuation_output_namespace` is the R2D.2 write destination.

For each team, Base and Advanced keys must match inside each complementary window. The early/late union is compared with the authenticated direct full-season Base/Advanced population. A valid recovered-only key proves the direct response non-exhaustive and freezes whole-team exclusion from the later final-test population. Zero recovered-only keys plus every frozen operational condition permits `operationally_resolved_no_observed_omission` and later inclusion of the direct full-season population; this is not proof of global exhaustiveness. Invalid or incomplete evidence leaves the team unresolved and blocks readiness. No recovered-only target is reconstructed, and no rating is aggregated.

The ignored write-once namespace is `cache/phase3f-r2d.2/protected-recovery-continuation/`. The checkpoint stops after acquisition, verification, reconciliation, disposition assignment, artifact hashing, and cache-only replay. Final-test construction, player-profile joins, preprocessing, estimator operations, predictions, metrics, and model serialization are prohibited.
