# Phase 3F-R0 final-training and final-test policy

## Status and boundary

This is a pre-result freeze. It authorizes deterministic reconstruction of final-training inputs and preprocessing from evidence already admitted to development. It does not authorize protected final-test acquisition, inspection, curation, model fitting, prediction, metric calculation, model serialization, production packaging, deployment, API work, or frontend work.

The protected final-test season remains `2025-26 Regular Season`. No Phase 3F-R0 operation may open a path or payload identified with that season, accept that season in an API parameter, inspect its availability or population, or make a network request.

The scientific record is preserved exactly:

1. Original R4: `INVALID EVALUATION — IMPLEMENTATION OR CONTRACT FAILURE`.
2. R4.1: `CORRECTION-ONLY RECONCILIATION FAILED — PRE-EVIDENCE GIT-STATUS PARSING FAILURE`.
3. R4.2: `VALID DEVELOPMENT-HOLDOUT PASS`.

R4.2 is the audit-cleared correction-only interpretation of the unchanged original prediction vector. The provenance waiver remains attached. The 2024-25 evidence is spent development evidence: it may enter final training and must never again be described as untouched evaluation evidence.

## Frozen model design

The sole final candidate remains:

- estimator: `sklearn.linear_model.Ridge`;
- alpha: `3000.0`;
- target: directly returned full-season pair `NET_RATING`;
- eligibility: directly returned full-season `POSS >= 150`;
- training weights: equal;
- predictors: the exact ordered 45-feature Phase 3D/R2 no-shot symmetric manifest;
- exact-250 rows: normally included unless a team-season is proven non-exhaustive;
- player history: nearest available profile strictly before the target season, at most three seasons back;
- confidence: `standard` for complete history and `lower` for one- or both-missing history;
- pair order: positive numeric player IDs in ascending canonical order;
- calibrator: none.

HGB, another alpha, an ensemble, a fallback estimator, target-derived predictors, exposure predictors, previous shared-pair-history predictors, and order-sensitive player-slot predictors are prohibited. Predictions must remain invariant under exchanging player slots. The favorable development result does not authorize redesign. Observed prediction compression remains a limitation to communicate later, not a basis for a post-hoc calibrator.

## Preprocessing contract

The required order is:

1. Assemble all 29,701 eligible rows in deterministic season/team/numeric-pair order.
2. Identify unique permitted selected player-season profiles represented by those rows.
3. Learn a single median per player-slot field from those unique expanded-training profiles.
4. Apply each learned value identically to either player slot.
5. Construct the 45 frozen symmetric features.
6. Learn any remaining symmetric-feature fill from expanded-training rows only.
7. Apply the frozen feature order.
8. Learn column means and population-standard-deviation scales from that one expanded matrix only, matching `StandardScaler` defaults (`ddof=0`, zero-variance scale `1.0`).
9. Freeze those values for the later protected final test.

The persisted matrix is explicitly **unscaled after both imputation stages**. `expanded_preprocessing_state.json` contains the one authorized scaler. A later runner must apply that scaler exactly once to training and test features. Independent test-set scaling and double scaling are integrity failures.

No protected final-test value may determine a median, fill, mean, scale, threshold, feature, eligibility rule, or population decision.

## Protected final-test population policy

The future population is predeclared without examining it:

- season/type: `2025-26 Regular Season`;
- target: directly returned full-season pair `NET_RATING`;
- eligibility: directly returned full-season `POSS >= 150`;
- identity: numeric canonical unordered pair within team and season;
- history priority: 2024-25, then 2023-24, then 2022-23;
- same-season complete player profiles: prohibited;
- previous shared-pair feature: prohibited;
- reconstructed or approximately aggregated targets: prohibited;
- partial-team evaluation after proven non-exhaustiveness: prohibited;
- exact-250 responses: flag and investigate, but do not automatically exclude;
- proven non-exhaustive team-season: exclude in full unless a definition-supported direct recovery source exists;
- excluded team disclosure: raw rows, eligible rows, and eligible possession cost;
- population decisions based on target magnitude or model error: prohibited;
- model or threshold change after result reveal: prohibited.

Population readiness requires all six evidence boundaries to pass independently:

1. **Predictor evidence completeness:** every permitted prior-profile asset is authorized, byte-verified, schema-approved, uniquely keyed, and resolves to the nearest strict-prior profile or an explicit missing-history state.
2. **Target evidence completeness:** every authorized direct full-season Base and Advanced body is byte-verified and reconciled, with no reconstructed rating.
3. **Team-population exhaustiveness:** all 30 team-seasons are adjudicated; capped responses are investigated; every proven non-exhaustive team is wholly excluded or directly recovered.
4. **Row eligibility:** direct finite target and possession fields, `POSS >= 150`, distinct positive numeric player IDs, and no target-dependent inclusion.
5. **Row alignment:** unique canonical observation keys, exact Base/Advanced reconciliation, pinned row order, exact feature order, and finite matrix values.
6. **Protected final-result reveal:** acquisition and reconciliation pass read-only audit before target/prediction co-opening or prediction generation is authorized.

The population is ready only when all six gates pass with immutable fingerprints, an exclusion ledger, and an empty execution namespace.

## Final training and final-test execution

### Stage A — evidence and implementation integrity

Before any model operation, verify:

- committed source and policy hashes;
- expanded-training artifact and preprocessing hashes;
- every authorized protected cache fingerprint;
- final-test completeness, recovery, and exclusion decisions;
- observation identity, uniqueness, alignment, numeric pair order, feature order, and finiteness;
- that protected targets contributed to neither preprocessing nor training;
- the exact frozen estimator configuration;
- an empty execution namespace;
- network prohibition during execution;
- absence of an alternate estimator, alpha, calibrator, or prediction path.

Any failure yields `INVALID`, preserves immutable failure evidence, and stops before model construction or operation.

### Stage B — exactly one final model operation

After Stage A passes:

1. Apply the frozen scaler exactly once.
2. Train exactly one Ridge estimator with alpha `3000.0` on all 29,701 rows with equal row weights.
3. Generate exactly one protected final-test prediction vector.
4. Derive exactly one baseline as the full-precision unweighted mean target across all and only the 29,701 training rows, then assign that constant to every eligible final-test row.
5. Persist the final prediction vector only after all pre-result gates pass.

The baseline may not use a protected target. Its numerical value need not be exposed in R0 and is not calculated in the human policy; its later derivation must be deterministic and hash-pinned.

A second model training operation, second prediction operation, alternate path, network access, or recovery-by-rerun is prohibited. A failure yields `INVALID`, quarantines partial outputs, preserves all events, and requires a new audited authorization before any retry.

### Stage C — metrics and classification

Calculate every frozen metric and diagnostic, assign the classification mechanically, and preserve unfavorable as well as favorable results. No tuning, calibration, retraining, transformation, or rerun is permitted after results are known. Failure of a mandatory metric or integrity check yields `INVALID` while preserving partial evidence.

Primary metrics are Ridge MAE, baseline MAE, baseline-minus-Ridge MAE improvement, Ridge RMSE, baseline RMSE, and baseline-minus-Ridge RMSE improvement.

Required diagnostics are R²; Spearman correlation; prediction-minus-target bias; prediction SD; target SD; prediction/target SD; possession-weighted Ridge and baseline MAE/RMSE; complete/one-missing/both-missing counts and errors; team counts, MAE, RMSE, bias, and exposure; leave-one-team-out descriptive diagnostics without retraining; exact-250 subgroup if applicable; deterministic ten-bin calibration; lower- and upper-tail target-minus-prediction differences; residual relationships with predictions and possessions; and comparison with historical chronological validation and the spent 2024-25 development result.

Diagnostics cannot override the classification.

## Final classification

- `INVALID`: any mandatory integrity stage fails, or either required improvement is nonfinite.
- `FINAL SCIENTIFIC PASS`: MAE improvement is at least `0.10` and RMSE improvement is at least `0.0`.
- `FINAL SCIENTIFIC FAILURE`: MAE improvement is less than or equal to `0.0`.
- `FINAL MIXED RESULT`: every other finite case.

The `0.10` MAE gate is the project's established materiality band, not a universal law. Nonnegative RMSE improvement protects against worsening large misses. Neither threshold is derived from protected final-test evidence. No diagnostic can override the result.

## Post-result rule

A pass permits consideration of production packaging; it does not authorize deployment. A mixed result requires a user decision about usefulness and more cautious product claims. A failure prohibits presenting the model as final-test validated.

Any redesign after opening protected targets converts that season into development evidence. The redesigned model requires a later untouched final test for an equivalent validation claim. Quiet retuning and reevaluation on the same targets are prohibited.
